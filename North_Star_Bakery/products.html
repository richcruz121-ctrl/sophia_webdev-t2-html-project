Breads, Pastries, and Cakes

Pick up a favorite or try something new. The selection changes with each batch, so send us a request if you have something in mind.

Breads

Our breads are made for morning toast, packed lunches, and sharing at the table. Loaves range from $6 to $10 each.

  • Country sourdough: A crusty loaf with a mild tang.
  • Whole wheat: A hearty choice for everyday sandwiches.
  • Baguette: A crisp loaf to serve alongside dinner.

Pastries and cookies

Choose a buttery croissant, a seasonal muffin, or a chocolate chip cookie. Individual treats range from $3 to $6.

For office meetings or family gatherings, ask us about a mixed box and let us know how many people you're serving.

Cakes

Simple celebration cakes are available in vanilla or chocolate. Prices range from $30 to $65, depending on size and decoration.

Include your pickup date, serving count, and any message you'd like on the cake when you contact us.

Planning a preorder

Prices are estimates. We'll confirm availability and the final price before accepting your order. No payment is taken here.
